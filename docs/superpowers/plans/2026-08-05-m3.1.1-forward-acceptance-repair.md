# M3.1.1 Forward Acceptance Repair Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (\`- [ ]\`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Repair the M3.1.1 forward-acceptance boundary so independently auditable M2.1.1 inputs, one-shot validator execution, fresh-context result records, and exact-HEAD CI can determine whether M3 may close without rewriting historical evidence.

**Architecture:** Keep the accepted M3 validator hardening and deterministic replay intact. Add a fixed-path one-shot validator runner, a read-only upstream-input audit, and an immutable fresh-result audit. Store revision-two inputs and results outside the installable Skill, preserve revision-one failures, and make CI verify saved artifacts without generating or repairing fresh outputs.

**Tech Stack:** Python standard library, unittest, UTF-8 JSON without BOM, SHA-256 byte hashing, PowerShell, GitHub Actions, and the existing M1/M2/M3 validators.

## Global Constraints

- Work only on branch codex/m3.1.1-acceptance-hardening, starting from exact HEAD 02d791275e6a8da16655a57aec5188d606c49357.
- Do not modify main, merge branches, implement M4, execute routes, experiments, simulations, training, downloads, services, uploads, deployment, or resource allocation.
- Preserve accepted M1/M2 evidence and all revision-one M3 forward failures; never overwrite or relabel blocked, NOT_RUN, or invalid evidence.
- New M2 inputs must be immutable files prepared from the accepted M2.1.1 baseline in a separate worktree and must not read M3 validator source, M3 tests, M3 fixtures, or prior fresh outputs.
- Each fresh case may read only the Skill, named references, one immutable M2 input, the frozen prompt, its output path, and the one-shot runner command. Finalize once and invoke the validator once.
- No fresh output may be repaired or retried after failure. Closure audits may verify saved bytes and hashes but may not regenerate fresh outputs.
- Do not claim empirical performance, transfer success, route execution, operational readiness, or a nuclear safety conclusion from structural validation.
- Require explicit formal user confirmation for any changed direction, minimum decisive test, or pre-confirmation bundle. Do not infer confirmation from an old event.
- Keep M3 IN_PROGRESS and M4 NOT_STARTED until F01/F02/F04/F05 are valid, F03 has the exact expected fail-closed result, the final closure commit exact-HEAD CI succeeds, and the worktree is clean.
