**Goal:** Advance the remotely verified r5.1-f02 readiness evidence from `READY_FOR_FRESH_AUTHORIZATION` to a narrowly bounded `READY_FOR_ONE_SHOT_FRESH_EXECUTION` state without launching or consuming a fresh run.

**Architecture:** Preserve the readiness manifest and dispatcher as immutable predecessor evidence. CI invokes only the read-only preflight.
